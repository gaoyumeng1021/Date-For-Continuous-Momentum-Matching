# Date-For-Continuous-Momentum-Matching
This compressed archive contains three parts，each of which corresponds to Figures 2, 3 and 4 in our work titled "Continuous Momentum Matching and Orbital Origin of Mini-Gaps in van der Waals Bilayers."

FIG. 2: Crystal structure (POSCAR) of the \(\sqrt{7}\times\sqrt{7}\) twisted \(h\)-BN bilayer supercell at \(21.8^{\circ}\);
FIG. 3: Crystal structure (POSCAR) of the rectangular \(h\)-BN bilayer supercell with \(11\times\sqrt{3}\) (top) and \(10\times\sqrt{3}\) (bottom) unit cells;
FIG. 4: Crystal structure (POSCAR) of the nearly quasicrystalline \(h\)-BN bilayer supercell of \((4\sqrt{3}\times4\sqrt{3})R30^{\circ} / (7\times7)R0^{\circ}\) with \(1\%\) strain.
